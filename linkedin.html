A thoughtful approach

Personal by design.

My Heart Treasury is built as an installable web app. Its saved entries are stored in the browser’s local IndexedDB storage on the device. A PIN can be enabled for additional protection, and users can create their own backups.

A note about your data: Saved entries are kept in the browser on the device where they are created. Users should export a backup before changing devices, clearing browser data, or uninstalling the app. Google Drive backup is optional and requires the user to authorize it.
